## Questions for Evaluation

Please review this brief and provide feedback on:

1. **Technical Completeness:** Are all technical aspects clearly explained?
2. **Patentability:** Does this appear to be novel and non-obvious?
3. **Prior Art Concerns:** Any red flags regarding existing patents/publications?
4. **Commercial Viability:** Is the business case compelling?
5. **Strategic Value:** How defensible and valuable is this IP?
6. **Gaps:** What information is missing or needs clarification?
7. **Improvements:** How can this brief be strengthened?
