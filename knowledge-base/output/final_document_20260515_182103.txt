## Issue 3: Field 3 — "Consistent Output Quality" Claim

**Observation:** The draft states we "ensure consistent output quality." The justification mentions PLC-controlled feedback loops adjusting based on syngas calorific value.

**Remaining Question:** At TRL 3, have these feedback loops been:
- (a) Designed on paper?
- (b) Simulated in process modeling software?
- (c) Demonstrated in bench-scale testing?

**Why This Matters:** "Ensure" is a strong word. If we're at design stage, "designed to maintain" is more defensible. If we have simulation data showing the control logic works, "ensure" may be acceptable.

**My Recommendation:** Unless there's simulation or empirical data, soften to:

> "We address ag-waste variability through modular process control (temp/residence time) **designed to maintain** consistent output quality for industrial uptake."
