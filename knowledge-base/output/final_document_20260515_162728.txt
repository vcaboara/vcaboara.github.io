## Recommended Refinements

### 1. Add "LCA-Ready" Signal (per Technical Evaluator)
**Current:** "...aligned with CORSIA and IMO 2050 decarbonization mandates."

**Revised:** "...aligned with CORSIA and IMO 2050 decarbonization mandates. LCA-ready methodology for third-party CI verification."

*Rationale:* Norway's GPFG has rejected investments for lacking verifiable carbon accounting. This one phrase pre-empts that objection.

### 2. Strengthen Feedstock Flexibility Signal
**Add to Technical Differentiators:**
- **Feedstock Agnostic Architecture:** Validated for high-silica (rice husk), high-lignin (forestry residue), and variable-moisture inputs — enabling deployment across diverse regional agricultural profiles.

*Rationale:* GIC/Temasek manages assets across Southeast Asia (palm waste, rice, rubber). Saudi PIF/NEOM needs desert-compatible inputs. One sentence dramatically expands addressable market.

### 3. Licensing Model Clarification
**Consider adding under Phase 2 or as new bullet:**
- **Licensing Model:** Configuration and process licensing only — no hardware manufacturing. Enables rapid deployment using regionally available industrial equipment suppliers.

*Rationale:* SWFs fund infrastructure, not R&D labs. Clarifying that this is "shovel-ready IP" rather than "prototype seeking manufacturing partner" changes the investment thesis entirely.
